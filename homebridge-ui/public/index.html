ADB enables monitoring native apps like Netflix. To enable:

  1. On your TV, go to Settings → System → About
  2. Tap "Android TV OS build" 7 times to unlock Developer Options
  3. Go back to Settings → System → Developer Options
  4. Enable Wireless Debugging

📱 Adding Accessories to the Apple Home App:
1. Playback Status / Volume Lightbulb (ADBCast Bridge):

In Apple Home, tap + → Add Accessory → Scan the ADBCast Bridge QR code or tap More options... to pair the Child Bridge.

2. Television Controls & Remote:

In Apple Home, tap + → Add Accessory → tap More options... → select your TV (e.g. Google TV) under Nearby Accessories and enter your Homebridge PIN.

Restart Homebridge to apply your changes and activate the child bridge and TV accessories.
